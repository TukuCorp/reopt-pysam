## 2) Business-model notes, one line each

- LevelTen: two-sided marketplace, monetises the SELL side through subscriptions plus per-deal transaction fees, buyer acquisition free, Asset plus Credit marketplaces reuse the rail, ReSolve adds analytics upsell.
- Pexapark: data and subscription (PPA Times, risk and price tooling) plus bespoke advisory and tender mandates, no public rate card for advisory.
- Zeigo and Schneider SB: SaaS (Zeigo Power, Hub, Network) plus buyer-paid consulting, RFP success fee shifted to winning developer is a procurement-design choice, not a published card, no Asia or Vietnam fee disclosure found.
- Xpansiv CBL: exchange and registry toll, seat plus per-unit retirement plus product fees, classic micro-fee high-volume model.

- Evident and I-REC: registry toll, fixed registrant and device and trader fees plus tiny per-MWh issuance, redemption and platform fees, cost-recovery not advisory.
- Ever.green: curated tax-credit deals, takes cut of facilitation economics (7 percent of transaction fees per ToS via Baker Tilly), closest public percent to a platform take-rate in this sweep.
- IEX: regulated-exchange toll per kWh plus per REC certificate, transparent because regulated.
- UK brokers: supplier-paid uplifts hidden in unit rates until Ofgem Oct-2024 forced disclosure, bands persist because pricing is unregulated but now visible.
- CRESS and TNB: single-buyer grid access charge, not a broker fee, included to stop false equivalence.
- EU data houses, Statkraft and Axpo origination, FlexiDAO and Granular, APAC utilities and platforms, US advisors: all searched-empty on numbers, bespoke or embedded-spread models with no public card.

## 3) Synthesis vs 2 percent of capex, repo FX math

Anchor: data/vietnam/vn_deal_defaults_2026.json, vnd_per_usd is 26400. Base 700 USD per kWp. CF base 17.5 percent with 9 percent low-yield sensitivity. 20 years. CRF at 9 percent over 20 years is 0.10955, recomputed live.
- 2 percent capex is 14 USD per kWp, which is 14000 USD per MWp. Annualised: 14000 times 0.10955 is 1533.65 USD per MWp per year.
- Base energy: 1 MWp times 8760h times 17.5 percent is 1533 MWh per year. Fee is 1533.65 divided by 1533, which is 1.0004 USD per MWh, about 26.4 VND per kWh.
- Low-yield at 9 percent CF: 788.4 MWh per year, about 1.95 USD per MWh, about 51 VND per kWh. Sensitivity scales linearly with capex per kWp input.
- Comparators: I-REC all-in registry (about 0.09-0.105 EUR per MWh, about 0.10 USD per MWh) is about one-tenth of the Schneider term. Ever.green 7 percent is a percent of facilitation fees on a different base, not comparable to percent of capex. IEX 4 paise per kWh (0.04 INR per kWh, about 0.48 USD per MWh) is about one-half of the Schneider term. UK broker 0.5p per kWh (about 5 GBP per MWh, about 6.5 USD per MWh) is about 6 times the Schneider term, and 1-2p caps (about 13-26 USD per MWh) are far above. CRESS 20 sen per kWh (about 200 MYR per MWh, about 42 USD per MWh) is a grid charge about 40 times, not a broker norm.
- Reading: the 2 percent capex term (about 1 USD per MWh base) sits ABOVE pure registry tolls, BELOW UK retail broker uplifts, and near regulated-exchange tolls. So it is not an outlier as a one-time signing fee level, but it IS an outlier in BASE (capex vs energy) and TIMING (lump at signing, developer-pays in a buyer-side RFP).
